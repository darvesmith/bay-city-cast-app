Support CAST

CAST is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit — ticket sales cover only part of what it costs to keep live theatre in Bay City. Here's how you can help:

  • Give. Make a one-time or monthly gift. GivingTuesday is Tuesday, December 1 — the same night as our Season Premiere — and our goal is $10,000.

  • Become a patron. Patron packages from $150 to $3,000 support the whole season and come with tickets to every regular production.

  • Volunteer. Usher, help with concessions, or lend a hand backstage — see the volunteer signup on the Home tab.

Donate to CAST

Donations are made through Zeffy. At checkout, Zeffy asks for a voluntary contribution to Zeffy — you can change or remove it, and CAST still receives the full amount of your gift.

Advertise in the CAST Playbills

Every audience member gets a playbill — and takes it home. Put your business in front of Bay City and Matagorda County audiences at every performance.

  • In every playbill. CAST prints a playbill for each production — A Christmas Carol's runs 16 pages — and audiences read them before the show, at intermission, and again at home.

  • A local audience. Our intimate 99-seat house fills with neighbours, families, and patrons from across Matagorda County — the same people who shop and dine locally.

  • Plan ahead. Playbills go to the printer about a week before opening night, so get in touch early to reserve your spot for the next production.

Ad sizes, current rates, and deadlines vary by production — email us and we'll send the details for the next playbill.

How to Sponsor a Show

Sponsoring a production helps CAST cover the real costs of putting a show on stage — royalties, scripts and materials, sets, costumes, and printing — while putting your name in front of the whole audience.

  • 1. Pick a show. Choose a production from the season that fits your business or family — a musical, a comedy, or a family favourite.

  • 2. Get in touch. Email CAST and we'll talk through sponsorship levels, what your support covers, and how you'd like to be recognised.

  • 3. Be part of the show. Sponsors are thanked in the playbill for their production, so every audience member sees the local support that made the show possible.

Season sponsorships are also available if you'd like to support the whole year. Every sponsorship is arranged personally — no forms, just a conversation.
